# Between — design decisions (2026-11-21)

Previewed in: Ink & butter, light
Source: docs/design/redesign/studio.html

## Home · Today
Which home layout should Between lead with?

- **Chosen:** D · The Deck (round 2)
  - One moment at a time as a card deck; the next two peek behind
  - Primary action sits in the thumb zone, with a ‘Later’ escape
  - Vibes shrink to two pills; the pet gives a single line of voice

## Welcome
Which first impression?

- **Chosen:** A · Headline first (round 1)
  - Display-xl headline with an italic ‘for two’
  - The pet holding a note, with a hand-written scrap and a taped photo around it
  - Three clear doors: start, join, sign in

## Know · quizzes
How should the Know tab open?

- **Chosen:** A · Library (round 1)
  - Daily card with the ‘?’ creature peeking over the edge
  - Resume row with partner status and progress
  - Filter chips stay quiet until selected

## Notes
Pinboard or correspondence?

- **Chosen:** B · Correspondence (round 2)
  - A thread of paper notes: hers on the left, yours on the right
  - The sealed note sits at the end of the thread, waiting
  - Docked compose bar, so writing back is always one tap away

## Memories
Scrapbook feed or month mosaic?

- **Chosen:** B · Month mosaic (round 2)
  - A calendar where every day with a page shows its photo
  - Today is a dashed cell, an open invitation
  - Tap a day for a preview card with both authors

## Plans
Ticket list or next-up boarding pass?

- **Chosen:** B · Next up (round 2)
  - The next plan as a boarding pass with a countdown
  - Upcoming plans as a date column; dreams as chips
  - Done count stays a quiet link

## Pet room
Pet on a rug, or a room of its own?

- **Chosen:** B · Room scene (round 2)
  - A full-bleed room with a window: Mochi has a place
  - Well-being as three compact columns
  - Feed / Play / Pet docked as buttons, Feed as the primary

## Check-in
Quick sheet or a full-screen moment?

- **Chosen:** B · Full screen (round 2)
  - The chosen mood is large and centred, so it feels considered
  - All 12 moods on one swipeable shelf
  - Privacy choice sits right above the save button

## Settings
Which settings layout?

- **Chosen:** B · Inset groups (round 2)
  - Opens on the two of you, not an account
  - Rows sit in rounded groups with coloured icon tiles
  - Destructive actions get a group of their own, last

## Navigation
Five tabs or four?

- **Chosen:** A · 5 tabs (today) (round 1)
  - Every pillar has a tab
  - Plans and Know compete with Notes for attention
