# The pet can't be neglected into distress

Pet mood and well-being are derived at read time from when the pet was last cared for; nothing decays in the background and there is no failure state. Well-being states fill with care and settle to "calm", never to empty or sad, and Bond never decreases. The product rule is "the pet grows because you spend time together", not "open the app or else", so classic draining meters were rejected even though they're the genre default.
