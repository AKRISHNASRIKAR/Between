# Love Notes

A private, shared little world for exactly two people: they check in on how they feel, learn about each other, leave things for each other, keep what happened, plan what's next, and look after a small creature together.

## People and the space

**Space**:
The private world two people share; everything else belongs to exactly one Space.
_Avoid_: couple, room, group, relationship

**Member**:
One of the (at most two) people in a Space.
_Avoid_: user (outside auth), account, participant

**Partner**:
From one Member's point of view, the other Member.
_Avoid_: other user, spouse, significant other

**Invite**:
A single-use, expiring code (or link) one Member shares so the other can join the Space.
_Avoid_: invitation link, pairing code

**Closed space**:
A Space someone has left; read-only for both Members for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
_Avoid_: archived, deleted space, breakup

## The pet

**Pet**:
The small creature both Members look after together; it belongs to the Space, never to one Member.
_Avoid_: dog, Mochi (as a concept), Tamagotchi, avatar

**Species**:
The kind of Pet (today only dog); decides how the Pet looks, not how it behaves.
_Avoid_: breed, type

**Stage**:
How grown-up the Pet is: egg, baby, young, grown. Stages only move forward.
_Avoid_: level, evolution

**Hatching**:
The moment the egg becomes a baby Pet, which happens when the second Member joins.
_Avoid_: birth, unlock

**Bond**:
A never-decreasing measure of shared care, earned from shared activity and never shown as a number.
_Avoid_: XP, score, points, love meter

**Pet mood**:
How the Pet feels right now (sleepy, content, happy, excited, peckish), derived from recent care and never stored.
_Avoid_: status, health

**Well-being**:
Three soft states (fullness, energy, love) that fill with care and settle back to calm, never to empty.
_Avoid_: stats, meters, hunger bar, needs

**Care**:
Feeding, petting or playing with the Pet.
_Avoid_: interaction (in product copy), chores, tasks

**Milestone**:
A good-news moment in the Pet's life (hatching, a new Stage, a first shared thing, a Bond threshold), recorded once.
_Avoid_: achievement, badge, trophy, streak

**Pet timeline**:
The Pet's own story: Milestones and recent Care, shown in the Pet room.
_Avoid_: activity feed, log, history

## Feeling

**Vibe**:
A Member's mood for their own local day, private unless they share it.
_Avoid_: mood (for the record itself), status, check-in (as a noun)

**Shared vibe**:
A Vibe its Member has chosen to show their Partner; a private Vibe is indistinguishable from no Vibe.
_Avoid_: public mood

**Observation**:
A gentle, hand-written line shown when both Members have shared a Vibe that day.
_Avoid_: insight, analysis, diagnosis

## Knowing

**Quiz pack**:
A themed set of questions from the content library.
_Avoid_: quiz (for the content), game, test

**Quiz session**:
One playthrough of a Quiz pack (or the Daily question) by both Members.
_Avoid_: attempt, round

**Daily question**:
One question per Space per day, played like a one-question Quiz session.
_Avoid_: question of the day, prompt

**Guess**:
A Member's prediction of their Partner's answer to a choice question.
_Avoid_: prediction (in copy)

**Reveal**:
The moment both Members' answers become visible, only after both have finished.
_Avoid_: results screen, score

## Leaving and keeping

**Note**:
Something one Member leaves for the other, written on paper and delivered by the Pet.
_Avoid_: message, chat, DM, love note (as a type name)

**Journal page**:
A dated page both Members can add to, each in their own Block.
_Avoid_: entry, post, diary entry

**Block**:
One Member's contribution to a Journal page: text, photos, or both; only its author can change it.
_Avoid_: comment, reply, paragraph

**Memory**:
A photo on a Journal page, seen through the photo view.
_Avoid_: upload, image, media (in copy)

**Future item**:
Something the two want to do together, which can be stamped done.
_Avoid_: dream (as the type name), goal, bucket-list item, todo

## Signals

**Moment**:
A small, time-sensitive thing surfaced on Today (a Note waiting, a Reveal ready).
_Avoid_: task, reminder, notification (on screen)

**Pet update**:
A notification in the Pet's voice about good news in the Pet's life; opt-in and at most one a day.
_Avoid_: pet alert, reminder, nudge
